## 4. Important fit discoveries

### Water tower

The Historic Desert Ruin Tower Modular Set is listed at only:

`1.14 × 2.97 × 1.32 m`

It cannot serve as the 12–15 m functional water tower without extreme scaling
or implausible repetition. It is rejected for that primary role.

The plan now retains the verified graybox water-tower structure and skins/caps
it with official surfaces and selected utility details. Quixel's free Metal
Water Tank is also too small to become the 5–7 m main tank, but it can serve as
a secondary tank at the tower compound or pump station.

### Sandbag cover

Military Trenches Barrier Sandbag Canvas Worn is:

`0.39 × 3.21 × 0.87 m`

This is an excellent match for planned waist-high fortified cover and can be
used at nearly its listed scale.

Military Trenches Barrier Sandbag Canvas Square 02 is:

`1.02 × 1.77 × 0.55 m`

It works as low cover or a cluster end piece, but not as full standing cover.

### Bazaar and salvage panels

The free Military Trenches corrugated wall is only 1.54 m high. It can form
partial patched construction or sit on a masonry base.

The paid Urban Shanty Town sheet is 2.71 m on its long dimension and is the
better direct fit for 2.0–2.6 m fences and market walls.

### Tea House door

Residential South Asian Door Wood Old is:

`0.93 × 0.99 × 2.36 m`

It closely matches the planned 1.0–1.2 m clear width and 2.1–2.4 m doorway
height. It remains localized to the Tea House so the entire town does not adopt
an overly South Asian decorative language.

### Unpublished dimensions

The Pakistan Street modular wall and window listings do not publish physical
dimensions. They remain visual candidates, not dimensionally approved assets.
Their purchase should wait until a downloadable file, reliable scale reference
or staging audit resolves their bounds.

## 5. Nanite decisions

- Dense closed or open photogrammetry meshes: provisional Nanite `yes`.
- Sandbags and open scans: Nanite render mesh with simple custom collision.
- Surface materials and decals: Nanite is not applicable.
- Interactive door: Nanite `no` initially; reconsider only if it remains a
  rigid supported mesh.
- Tiny storage box: Nanite is unnecessary.
- City Sample vehicles: likely suitable for Nanite as static render meshes, but
  the selected vehicle components require staging verification.

## 6. Purchase rule

Nothing is acquired solely because it appears on this shortlist.

Before purchase:

1. Confirm the signed-in price and license.
2. Confirm the publisher still reads `Epic Games` or `Quixel Megascans`.
3. Compare listing previews against the Sunscar palette.
4. Confirm the asset fills a role not already solved by a free selection.
5. Confirm its scale or record the unresolved scale explicitly.
6. Obtain approval for the exact paid list and total.
