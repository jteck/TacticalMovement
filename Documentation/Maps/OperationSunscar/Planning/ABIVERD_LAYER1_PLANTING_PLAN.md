# Abiverd - Layer 1 shelterbelt planting plan

Generated 2026-08-25. 8 m spacing, 9 m off centreline, min 3.5 m from any site footprint.
Trunk proxy validated at 9-14 m height, 35-55 cm diameter.

**Total: 134 trees.** Species per plan 1.0: Lombardy poplar (Megascans Trees, NOT
Megaplants - those need Procedural Vegetation Editor + Nanite Foliage Experimental,
which rules 6/7 forbid in the core). Tamarisk/saltcedar at canal edges.

## Measured effect - validated with proxies BEFORE purchase

| Metric | Without trees | With 134 trees |
|---|---|---|
| Cover objects >=110 cm | 547 | 694 |
| Median distance to cover | 10.0 m | **7.1 m** |
| Within 12 m of cover | 83.2% | **89.9%** |
| Site-to-site clear pairs | 13 (11%) | 10 (8%) |
| Longest sightline | 126 m | **126 m - unchanged** |

Trees serve the **cover rhythm**, not sightlines. Long lines run between sites, not
along canal/roads - the koshk masses already address those.

134 trees (8 m) vs 182 (6 m) made no measurable difference: median 7.1 m either way.
Density is dominated by ruins/bunds/walls; trees only matter locally along their lines.

## Acceptance (plan Layer 1)

- [ ] Trunks have collision and are recorded as cover
- [ ] Seasonal variants share identical trunk geometry and transforms
- [ ] Cull distances set and recorded
- [ ] Non-Nanite in the core
